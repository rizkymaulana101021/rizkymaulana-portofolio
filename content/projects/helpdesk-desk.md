**The problem:** every IT request arrived by email, chat, or a tap on the shoulder. Nothing was tracked, so nothing was learned.

**What I built:** a single-form intake that asks three questions (what's broken, how bad, who's blocked), a kanban board for the support team, and a weekly digest that surfaces the top recurring issues.

**Why it worked:** I wrote it *after* a year of answering those tickets by hand. The form asks what a technician actually needs — not what a vendor's template thinks you need.
